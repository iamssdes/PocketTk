PocketTk
========

1. Drag PocketTk into the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications.

If macOS says it can't verify the app (this build is not notarized by Apple):
  - macOS 14 or older: right-click PocketTk, choose Open, then Open again.
  - macOS 15 or newer: try to open it once, then go to
    System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway".

The first time PocketTk starts it installs what it needs (a Java JDK and Google's Android build tools) into
~/.pockettk. You are asked to accept Google's Android SDK license; no administrator password is needed.
